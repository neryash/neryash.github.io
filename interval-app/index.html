What's Interval?

Interval gives you a different way to handle tasks. Some tasks can't be assigned to an exact time, or date, and need to be completed periodically in a less (or more) strict manner.

Interval let's you define tasks and show you the ones that need to be completed on top, while storing a log of all your completed tasks in a calendar.

What Are Tasks, And What Are Goals?

Interval's todo method let's you set tasks and get a periodic reminder for them, while goals are more long term. Goals can be forever (e.g. daily Tasks, Study French...), or have a time limit (e.g. Run the Boston Marathon).

A task can be part of a goal, and in Interval's main screen, you can sort tasks by goals.

What Happens if I'm Late to a Task?

It happens! We've all been there, you couldn't complete your pull workout because your child is sick, or you were on call. We get it, you're human. Forget rescheduling all your exercise days and reorganizing them, Interval shows you the exercise you need to do based on it's frequency.
